# Questions

## Main Questions

1. Is this full-time (40 hours/week)?
   - Yes, it is a full-time сontractor-based position (we don’t have an office in Argentina), around 40 hours per week.

2. Is the contract possibly long-term?
   - Yes. We do not have a fixed end date for the contract. We expect a long-term cooperation, ideally at least one year.

3. How is performance evaluated?
   - You will have regular meetings with the client, and the client evaluates your performance. We are an outstaffing company, so we do not do micromanagement.

4. Is equipment provided or do I use my own? Do you provide any benefit regarding equipment?
   - Usually, our specialists use their own laptop. In some cases, equipment can be discussed with our CEO if needed.
     As for benefits, we offer:
     - Fully remote work with flexible hours (Monday–Friday, around 8 hours/day)
     - No time-tracking software
     - Fixed monthly salary in USD
     - Payment in the first week of each month
     - Payment via SWIFT/Wire, Wise, Deel, PayPal, or Payoneer
     - B2B contract with no fixed end date
     - 10 paid vacation days per year + unlimited unpaid leave upon agreement

5. Are there mandatory meetings outside normal business hours or on weekends?
   - No. We work Monday to Friday, and weekends are off. You will work about 8 hours per day with overlap in the US time zone. Any meetings are normally scheduled during working hours.

6. How many people will I be working with?
   - At `company-name`, we have around 15 team members, but they work on different projects. During the client interview, you will be informed which team you would join.
7. What is the budgeted salary range?
   - May I ask about your current salary or your salary expectations? Then I can check the client's budget and see if it matches your expectations.

8. Which payment platform is used? Considering I'm based in Argentina.
   - We can pay via SWIFT/Wire transfer, Wise, Deel, PayPal, or Payoneer, depending on what works best for you.

9. How frequently are contractors paid?
   Contractors are paid once a month, during the first week of each month.

## About the role

This is a Full-Stack Software Engineer (middle) position with `client` (our client), a US company that develops software for the hospitality industry, including business intelligence, hotel operations, and labor management platforms.

In this role, you will:

- Develop and maintain applications using C#/.NET and Angular
- Build and improve REST APIs and microservices
- Write automated tests
- Take part in architecture and technical discussions
- Review code and mentor junior engineers
- Work closely with Product, QA, DevOps, and other teams

The main technologies are C#, .NET, Angular, TypeScript, SQL, Azure/AWS, Git, and CI/CD. The role is fully remote and focused on building scalable, high-quality software.
