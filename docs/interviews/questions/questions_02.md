```txt
The recruiting interview will last about 45 minutes and will cover both
general and a few technical questions. The recruiter will also provide
detailed information about the vacancy, responsibilities, working conditions,
and the company.
```

Additional questions:

For a 45-minute recruiter interview, the most valuable questions are those that help you determine whether the opportunity aligns with your career goals, compensation expectations, and working style. Since this is a recruiter (not a technical interviewer), focus on the role, the company, the client, and the hiring process rather than deep technical architecture.

---

# Questions for the recruiter interview

---

## (1) About the project

- What is the main goal of this project?
- Is this a new product or an existing one?
- Who is the end client? (If it can be disclosed.)
- What industry does the client operate in?
- How large is the development team?
- How long has the project been running?
- Is this considered a long-term engagement?

---

## (2) Daily responsibilities

- What does a typical day look like for someone in this role?
- What percentage of the work is backend versus frontend?
- What are the main responsibilities during the first three months?
- What are the biggest challenges the new developer will face?

---

## (3) Technology stack

- Which versions of .NET and Angular are currently being used?
- Which cloud platform do you use (Azure, AWS, GCP)?
- What database technologies are involved?
- What CI/CD tools are used?
- Do you follow Clean Architecture, DDD, CQRS, or other architectural patterns?
- Is there any legacy code that the new hire will be maintaining?

---

## (4) Team structure

- Who would I report to?
- How many developers are on the team?
- Are there dedicated QA engineers?
- Are there DevOps engineers?
- Is there a Product Owner and Scrum Master?
- How often does the team meet?

---

## (5) Development process

- Which Agile methodology do you follow?
- How long are your sprints?
- How are code reviews conducted?
- What does your release process look like?
- How are tasks assigned?

---

## (6) Remote work

- Is the position fully remote?
- Are there required working hours or is the schedule flexible?
- Which time zones does the team work in?
- Are there any travel requirements?

---

## (7) Compensation and benefits

- What is the salary range budgeted for this position?
- Is compensation paid monthly?
- Is the salary fixed or performance-based?
- What payment methods are available?
- Are there paid vacation days?
- Are there paid holidays?
- Is paid sick leave included?
- Is there an annual salary review?
- Are bonuses offered?

---

## (8) Equipment

- Do you provide company equipment?
- If I use my own equipment, is there any reimbursement?
- Are there software licenses provided?

---

## (9) Contract

- Is this a contractor position or full-time employment?
- Which legal entity would I sign the contract with?
- Is there a probation period?
- How long is the contract expected to last?
- Is there an opportunity for extension?

---

## (10) Performance expectations

- What would success look like after six months?
- How is performance evaluated?
- What are the expectations during onboarding?

---

## (11) Company culture

- How would you describe Andersen's engineering culture?
- What do developers enjoy most about working here?
- What are the company's core values?
- How does the company support professional growth?

---

## (12) Career development

- Are there opportunities to move into senior or lead positions?
- Does the company support certifications?
- Is there a learning budget?
- Are developers encouraged to attend conferences or training?

---

## (13) Hiring process

- What are the next steps after this interview?
- How many interview stages are there?
- Will there be a coding assessment?
- Will there be a technical interview?
- Who will conduct the technical interview?
- Approximately how long does the hiring process take?
- When should I expect feedback?

---

# High-priority questions (recommended)

If time is limited, prioritize these:

1. What does a typical day in this role look like?
2. What percentage of the work is .NET versus Angular?
3. What technologies and versions are currently used?
4. What are the team's biggest technical challenges?
5. What is the salary range budgeted for this role?
6. Is the position contractor or full-time employment?
7. What are the expected working hours and time zone overlap?
8. How large is the team?
9. What are the next steps in the interview process?
10. What would success look like in the first six months?

These questions demonstrate genuine interest in the role while helping you evaluate whether the position is a good fit. They also cover the topics recruiters are typically prepared to answer.
