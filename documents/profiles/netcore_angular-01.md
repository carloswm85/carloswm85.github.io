# Senior Full Stack Engineer (.NET / Angular)

<https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/senior-full-stack-engineer-with-c%23-net-at-svitla-systems-inc-4436330066/>

---

## Requirements

### Required

- Strong experience as a Full Stack Engineer with a primary focus on **C#/.NET** backend development.
- Expert knowledge of **Angular (2+)** and **TypeScript**.
- Understanding of designing and maintaining **REST APIs**.
- Strong experience with **SQL** and relational databases.
- Experience maintaining and evolving existing enterprise applications.
- Experience working in **Agile** environments.

### Nice to Have

- Knowledge of **Snowflake** or other columnar data warehouses.
  - <https://www.snowflake.com/en/>
- Experience with cloud platforms (**AWS**, **Azure**, or **GCP**).
  - GCP: Google Cloud Platform - <https://cloud.google.com/>
- Familiarity with **CI/CD**, containers, and automated testing.
- Familiarity with AI-assisted software development tools, such as:
  - Claude Code
  - Cursor
  - GitHub Copilot

---

## Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain backend services using **C#/.NET** for an existing enterprise application.
- Build responsive, maintainable user interfaces with **Angular** and **TypeScript**.
- Design and support **REST APIs** and integrate them with relational databases.
- Develop, optimize, and troubleshoot SQL-based business logic supporting application workflows.
- Debug, refactor, and enhance existing code with a focus on:
  - Performance
  - Scalability
  - Maintainability
- Collaborate closely with Product, QA, and Engineering teams to deliver high-quality software.
