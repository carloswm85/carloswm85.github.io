# Senior Cloud Software Engineer (.NET)

**ARGENTINA**

Caylent is an AI-first cloud services company that helps organizations turn ambitious ideas into meaningful business impact. As an AWS Premier Tier Services Partner and a charter member of Anthropic’s Claude Partner Network, we combine deep expertise in AWS, artificial intelligence, and Anthropic’s Claude platform to help customers modernize their technology, build intelligent products, and move AI from experimentation into production.

Our capabilities span generative and agentic AI, cloud migration and modernization, cloud-native application development, data and analytics, DevOps, managed services, security and compliance, and customer experience transformation. At Caylent, our people always come first.

We are a fully remote global company with employees in Canada, the United States and Latin America. We celebrate the culture of each of our team members and foster a community of technological curiosity. Come talk to us to learn more about what it means to be a Caylien!

## The Mission

We are seeking an exceptional Senior Software Engineer with a strong .NET focus to join our growing Cloud Native Applications team.

Our immediate client needs require deep expertise in modernizing and building applications with .NET Framework, modern .NET, and C#. However, this is a broader software engineering role, not a position where you will work exclusively in .NET. Over time, you will have opportunities to work across different languages, frameworks, cloud-native architectures, and AI-enabled software solutions based on the needs of our customers and your interests.

The right candidate is someone who has deep curiosity and expertise in designing and building applications with .NET Framework and .NET (formerly Core), creating AI-first software solutions, and is passionate about working with our customers, partners, and colleagues to drive innovation forward.

Your mission will be to work alongside Caylent’s Software teams, Sales, and Project Managers to deliver AWS solutions across our diverse and forward-thinking customer base. You’ll be hands-on with the latest technologies and support customers looking to bring cutting-edge ideas to market.

## Your Assignment

- Collaborate as a member of software development teams to deliver production-ready client solutions, with an initial focus on .NET modernization.
- Work with the Lead Architect to review technical requirements, contribute to technical decision making, and deliver working software to clients.
- Participate in discovery sessions to gather customer requirements.
- Follow architecture decisions and adhere to the solution design created by the Lead Architect.
- Create, automate, and refine multiple levels of AI-driven testing.
- Collaborate with lead software architects, project managers, peers, and leaders to deliver value to clients.
- Expand your experience across additional languages, frameworks, AWS services, and AI technologies as customer engagements evolve.

## Your Qualifications

### Core Consulting Qualifications

#### Customer Experience

Deliver exceptional client experiences, respond professionally to customer inquiries, communicate project status, and provide valuable solutions aligned to client needs.

#### Collaboration

Demonstrate strong collaboration with internal teams, proactively aligning on priorities, responding to clients, and addressing client feedback.

#### Communication

Communicate technical information clearly and actively listen to clients and team members to confirm understanding and prevent miscommunication.

### Technical Qualifications

- Deep expertise in the .NET ecosystem
- C# at a senior level
- Windows Services → Docker/ECS containerization
- SQL Server proficiency
- Ability and interest in working with additional languages, frameworks, or application architectures on future projects

### Especially Valuable for Our Current Client Work

- Hands-on .NET Framework (v4.6/v4.8) experience
- Hands-on modern .NET Core / .NET 8–10 experience
- Proven ASP.NET WebForms → Blazor migration work
- NuGet incompatibility remediation experience
- IIS → Linux porting knowledge
- Windows Services → Docker/ECS containerization
- MSMQ → RabbitMQ messaging migration

### Preferred Qualifications

- Learning and building with AI both for productivity and for enhancing client solutions.
- Familiarity and experience with AWS cloud services.
- Familiarity with additional modern languages or frameworks such as Python, Node.js, React, TypeScript.
- Experience contributing and delivering on client-facing customer engagements.
- Experience with: UX/UI, LLMs, AgentCore, GenAI, automated testing, CI/CD, IaC
